## Snooping & Button Mapping Completion

### Background & Context
During reverse-engineering of the realme Buds Wireless 5 ANC (Model RMA2412 neckband), we confirmed:
- Query `0x0108` returns 5 baseline hardware button mappings:
  - `deviceType=1, button=1, action=1` (Single press) -> `function=1` (Play/Pause)
  - `deviceType=1, button=1, action=2` (Double press) -> `function=6` (Switch Device)
  - `deviceType=1, button=1, action=3` (Triple press) -> `function=5` (Noise Mode)
  - `deviceType=1, button=1, action=4` (Long press 1s) -> `function=3` (Previous Track)
  - `deviceType=1, button=4, action=1` (Button 4 single press) -> `function=8` (Volume Up)
- Write `0x0401` sends button remappings over the wire.
- Testing via `ButtonMappingPersistenceProbe` revealed that while some writes are accepted (`status=00`), certain actions do not persist across RFCOMM disconnect/reconnect or reset to baseline.
- Unsolicited notification `0x0501` is emitted by the headset; when payload contains byte `10` (`0x0A`), it indicates the "Ring Phone" action.
