Validated on Pixel 10 / Android 17 using official realme Link selections plus GET-only `0x0108` readback.

Confirmed MFB functions: None `0`, Play/Pause `1`, Voice Assistant `4`, Previous `5`, Next `6`, Game mode `17`. All four MFB gestures expose the same six choices. Confirmed baseline: Single `f1`, Double `f6`, Triple `f5`, Touch and hold `f1`; ANC button reports `d1/b4/a1/f8`. MFB writes persist across RFCOMM reconnect. The reported Volume+ mapping does not apply or persist and remains non-writable.

Official ANC-button choices are Switch noise control modes, Noise cancellation, Off, and Transparency. These choices leave `b4/a1/f8` unchanged, so their mode-list encoding remains unresolved and read-only.
